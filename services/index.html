Scan to BIM Services and Revit Modeling at ViBIM

Everything we deliver starts from the same input: a registered point cloud supplied by a survey or reality capture firm. What changes is which part of the building is modeled, to what level of detail, and what the model is used for afterwards. The services below are grouped that way. Each heading links to the full service page on our main website; where we have a summary on this site, it is linked underneath.

By discipline

Each discipline has its own team lead, and one project can cover a single discipline or all four on the same scan.

Architectural Scan to BIM Services

Walls, floors, ceilings, openings, roofs and facades modeled in Revit to the agreed LOD, for renovation and retrofit design. Summary: architectural modeling.

Structural BIM Modeling Services

Columns, beams, slabs, framing and foundations in steel, concrete or timber, with rebar where the scope asks for it. Summary: structural modeling.

MEP BIM Modeling Services

Ductwork, piping, cable tray and equipment with real sizes and connections, checked for clashes. Summary: MEP modeling.

Topography BIM Modeling Services

Terrain surfaces, contours and site context as a Revit base for site planning, with DWG site output. Summary: topography modeling.

By deliverable

These services sit on top of a model, either one we build from your scan or one you already have.

As-Built BIM Modeling Services

A model of the building as it was actually constructed, with rooms, spaces and asset parameters when needed. Summary: as-built modeling.

As-Built Drawing Services

Plans, sections and elevations taken from the model, to your title blocks and annotation standards, as Revit sheets, DWG or PDF.

Revit Family Creation Services

Custom parametric families, such as doors, windows, fixtures and equipment, modeled to manufacturer geometry and reusable across projects.

BIM for Facility Management Services

Asset data added to the Revit model in a COBie-ready structure, so owners can connect it to their CAFM or FM system at handover.

BIM Collaboration Using ACC

Models set up and maintained in Autodesk Construction Cloud, so distributed teams review, coordinate and track work in one place.
