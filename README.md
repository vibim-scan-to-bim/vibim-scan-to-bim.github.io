# ViBIM: Scan to BIM and Revit Modeling

ViBIM (Vietnam BIM Consultancy and Technology Application Company Limited) is a BIM outsourcing company founded in Hanoi in 2014. Our teams convert point clouds from laser scanning and survey firms into native Revit models across architecture, structure, MEP and topography.
